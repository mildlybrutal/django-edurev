# Quick-start guide

Sign in at the app's address with your institutional email. Use the sun, monitor or moon buttons in the header to choose light, system or dark theme. Recruiters use `/recruiter/login`. If your role requires two-factor authentication, you will be taken to **Account** to set it up first: add the shown key to an authenticator app and enter a code.

## Students

**New here?** If your institution allows it, create your account at `/signup`. The placement cell confirms the CGPA and backlogs you enter, and until they do, those figures are marked *self-declared*.

After you sign up, a pop-up asks for your GitHub and LeetCode usernames, your LinkedIn link and your resume (PDF, up to 5 MB). You can add them now or choose **Later**. Everything can be changed later under **My Profile**.

1. **Skills & Evidence:** add projects, certifications and coding-platform profiles. Tag the skills each one shows, with an honest level, and tick *Ask my mentor to verify it*.
   - Unverified evidence counts for less.
   - Coding-platform activity alone can't establish more than *Beginner*.
   - Assessments and mock interviews recorded by staff appear here automatically as verified.
2. **My Profile:** your academic record (read-only), profile-completion checklist, and skill profile. Select a skill to see exactly which evidence supports it.
   - **Coding activity:** your GitHub and LeetCode ratings (0–100), synced from public data. Select *How this is rated* to see each part of a rating. This is the fifth readiness dimension, and it counts as unverified evidence. LeetCode data comes from an unofficial source.
   - **Resume:** upload, replace, view or delete it. Your mentor and the placement cell can view it. A recruiter sees it only if the placement cell releases it for their drive.
3. **Placement Drives:** for each drive you see two separate things:
   - **Eligibility:** the company's hard criteria. Each one is ticked or crossed with the reason.
   - **Skill compatibility and readiness:** how your evidence matches the role, with the five readiness dimensions.

   Open a drive to see your **ranked skill gaps** with a suggested action for each, then **Register** if you are eligible and registration is open.
4. **Preparation Plan:** from a drive page, create a plan. It turns your gaps into dated tasks and checkpoints before the drive date. Tick items off as you go. You get a reminder the day before each checkpoint.
5. **Progress:** your readiness over time and plan progress.
6. **Dashboard:** your next steps in order. The violet **Next** item is the one to do first, and a time budget shows days left before the drive against tasks done. **Notifications** (top right) tell you about verifications, new drives that match you, eligibility, shortlists and checkpoints.

## Faculty

- **Verification Queue:** your mentees' evidence, oldest first. Check the link or reference, then **Verify**, or **Reject** with a reason (required).
- **Assessments & Mock Interviews:** record rubric scores (technical assessments name the skill) and mock-interview scores and feedback. They become verified evidence and update readiness.
- **Dashboard / My Students:** mentees sorted by lowest readiness, with each student's profile, readiness, evidence and assessments.

## Placement officers and head

1. **Placement Drives → New drive:** company, role, dates. The drive starts as a draft that students can't see.
2. **Job description tab:** paste the JD and select *Process JD*. Review the extracted skills (level, weight, mandatory) and eligibility criteria, untick anything wrong, and select *Apply to drive*. Processing is rule-based against the institution's skill taxonomy; there is no AI.
3. **Requirements & eligibility:** adjust requirements and criteria, or apply a role template, then select **Publish**. This opens registration, evaluates every student and notifies them.
4. **Matching & shortlist:** eligibility, skill match and readiness side by side, with filters. Select a student to see their full evaluation. After *Close registration*, set shortlist and selection decisions; students are notified.
5. **Supply vs demand** (drive tab), **Reports** and **Batch Analytics:**
   - which required skills the batch lacks, which tells training what to run next
   - readiness distribution, gap frequency and verification coverage
   - the batch-readiness job
   - department comparison and training effectiveness (head)
6. **Recruiter Access** (head only): create recruiter accounts and grant access to one drive. A grant sets an expiry, whether all registered students or only the shortlist are visible, and exactly which fields are released. Revoke at any time. Every recruiter view is in the **Access log**.

## Recruiters

Sign in at `/recruiter/login`. **Your drives** lists drives you currently have access to, with the fields released to you. Open a drive to search candidates by skill (e.g. "frontend framework" also finds React developers), filter, and open a candidate. You only see students registered for that drive and only the released fields. All activity is logged. There is no bulk export.
